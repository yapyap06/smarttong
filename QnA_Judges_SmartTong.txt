Q1. [HARDWARE RELIABILITY]
    The ultrasonic sensors used to measure bin fill level are known to give
    false readings when the waste surface is uneven, wet, or tilted (common
    in Malaysian weather). How does SmartTONG handle these erroneous readings,
    and what is your noise-filtering or calibration strategy?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    We apply a median-filter over a rolling window of 5 readings per sensor
    cycle to eliminate outlier spikes. If consecutive readings deviate more
    than +/-15% from the rolling average, the system flags a "sensor fault"
    state and falls back to the last valid reading while alerting the
    dashboard. Field calibration is performed on deployment and re-validated
    every 30 days.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q2. [AI MODEL ACCURACY]
    Your image classifier claims to distinguish recyclable categories. What
    is the confusion matrix result between similar-looking categories such as
    'clear plastic bottle' vs. 'opaque plastic container', and what is the
    overall top-1 accuracy on your test set?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    Our EfficientNetV2-B0 model achieves 91.4% top-1 accuracy on a held-out
    test set of 2,400 images across 6 classes. The most common confusion is
    between 'clear PET' and 'opaque HDPE' (~7% misclassification rate).
    We address this by displaying the top-3 predictions with confidence
    scores to the citizen, allowing manual correction before submission.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q3. [OFFLINE / CONNECTIVITY RESILIENCE]
    Many housing areas in Selangor, particularly low-cost flats (PPR) and
    rural Hulu Langat districts, have poor or no Wi-Fi/4G coverage. How
    does SmartTONG function when Internet connectivity is lost?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    The ESP32 firmware stores sensor readings locally in its EEPROM/SPIFFS
    for up to 72 hours and transmits in batch when connectivity is restored.
    For the citizen app, the AI classifier can run locally (on-device via
    TensorFlow.js or cached HF Space response). The dashboard goes into
    a "last known state" mode, clearly indicating stale data with a
    timestamp warning.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q4. [DATA SECURITY & PRIVACY]
    Citizen images uploaded for waste scanning could inadvertently capture
    people's faces or private property. How do you ensure user privacy and
    comply with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2010?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    Images are processed in-memory and are NOT stored permanently. The
    classifier returns only the prediction label and confidence score;
    raw images are discarded server-side immediately after inference.
    We display a clear privacy notice in the citizen app. No PII is
    collected. Our API endpoints use HTTPS and API-key authentication.

Q5. [SENSOR CALIBRATION — MQ-135]
    The MQ-135 gas sensor used for contamination detection requires a 24-hour
    warm-up and drifts significantly in high-humidity environments (Selangor
    averages 80-90% RH). What is your calibration protocol and how do you
    account for humidity-induced drift?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    We apply the Steinhart-Hart compensation equation using readings from a
    co-located DHT22 humidity/temperature sensor. The baseline (R0) is
    re-calibrated every power cycle in clean air. In field conditions,
    a monthly recalibration reminder is issued via the dashboard. A humidity
    threshold alert (>85% RH) is flagged so operators treat readings with
    caution.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q6. [REAL-TIME VS. BATCH PROCESSING]
    Your dashboard shows "real-time" bin status. What is the actual end-to-end
    latency from physical waste being deposited to the dashboard updating,
    and what happens to the truck route if a bin suddenly fills up mid-route?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    Sensor polling is every 30 seconds; Firebase Firestore pushes updates
    within ~1-2 seconds via WebSocket listeners. End-to-end latency is
    approximately 30-35 seconds. If a bin reaches the critical threshold
    mid-route, the route agent re-computes the circuit dynamically and
    pushes a new waypoint sequence to the truck driver's mobile dashboard
    without requiring a manual refresh.

Q7. [SCALABILITY OF IoT INFRASTRUCTURE]
    You have demonstrated 4-5 bins. Selangor has over 1 million waste bins
    across 12 local councils. How does your Firebase-based architecture scale
    to 10,000 or 100,000 connected bins without exponential cost growth?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    Firebase Firestore's pricing scales per document read/write. At 100,000
    bins x 48 updates/day = 4.8 million writes/day approx USD 2.88/day under
    current Spark pricing. We mitigate this by using delta-update strategy
    (only writing when readings change >5%) and batching writes every
    5 minutes. For large-scale deployment, we would migrate to a self-hosted
    TimescaleDB or AWS IoT Core for sub-cent-per-message pricing.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q8. [POWER CONSUMPTION — ESP32]
    ESP32 draws ~240 mA during Wi-Fi transmission. If bins are in locations
    without mains power (roadside, parks), how do you power the device,
    and what is the expected battery life?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    We use a 10,000 mAh LiPo battery paired with a 6W solar panel. With
    deep-sleep mode (drawing ~10 uA) between 30-second active cycles,
    estimated battery life is 14 days without solar input. In full sun
    (average 5 peak-sun-hours/day in Selangor), the panel fully offsets
    daily consumption. We include a low-battery alert at 20% charge.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q9. [FIRMWARE OTA UPDATES]
    How do you push firmware updates to deployed ESP32 units in the field
    without requiring physical access, and what is your rollback strategy
    if a faulty update bricks a device?

    MODEL ANSWER:
    We use ESP32 Arduino OTA (Over-the-Air) update triggered via MQTT
    command from the server. We maintain a dual-partition flash layout
    (active + standby), so if the new firmware fails to boot 3 times,
    the bootloader automatically reverts to the last stable partition.
    Update success rate is confirmed by a heartbeat message within 60 s.

     Selangor's existing GIS road data layers provided by JUPEM.

Q12. [SERVO MECHANISM DURABILITY]
     The automated bin lid uses a servo motor. Servos are mechanical
     components subject to wear, jamming, and failure. What is the MTBF
     (Mean Time Between Failure) of your servo mechanism, and what happens
     if the servo jams — does the bin become permanently locked?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     We use MG996R metal-gear servos rated for 100,000 cycles. At 50
     actuations/day, this translates to ~5.5 years MTBF. If servo torque
     feedback detects a jam (current draw exceeds threshold for >2 s),
     the system defaults to the OPEN position to prevent citizen lockout
     and immediately alerts maintenance. A manual override handle is also
     provided on the bin housing.

Q13. [GEMINI AI DEPENDENCY]
     Your SilaTanya chatbot relies on Google Gemini 2.5 Flash. What is your
     contingency if Google deprecates, rate-limits, or significantly
     price-increases this API? How do you avoid vendor lock-in?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     We abstract the LLM layer behind an API interface so we can swap
     providers (Gemini -> OpenAI GPT-4o -> local Ollama) with a single
     config change. Our SOP knowledge base is stored locally as a RAG
     vector store (ChromaDB), so the retrieval layer is always independent
     of the LLM. We also cache frequent Q&A pairs to reduce API calls by
     ~40%.

Q14. [BROADCAST CHANNEL — BROWSER LIMITATION]
     You use BroadcastChannel for cross-tab synchronisation. This only
     works within the same browser on the same machine. If a council officer
     uses a different device or browser, they won't receive real-time updates.
     How do you address multi-device, multi-user real-time synchronisation?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     BroadcastChannel is used as a local performance optimisation to reduce
     redundant Firebase reads within the same workstation. The canonical
     real-time state is always in Firebase Firestore, which pushes updates
     to ALL connected clients regardless of device or browser. The broadcast
     channel is supplementary, not the primary sync mechanism.

Q15. [CYBERSECURITY — ESP32 ATTACK SURFACE]
     ESP32 devices on public Wi-Fi networks are vulnerable to deauthentication
     attacks, man-in-the-middle attacks, and credential theft. How do you
     secure the IoT communication layer?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     All MQTT/HTTP traffic uses TLS 1.2+ with certificate pinning. Wi-Fi
     credentials are stored in ESP32's NVS (Non-Volatile Storage) partition
     which is encrypted with eFuse keys. We use a dedicated IoT VLAN
     separate from public Wi-Fi. Firebase Security Rules enforce that only
     authenticated service accounts can write sensor data. Regular security
     audits are planned pre-deployment.

     MODEL ANSWER:

     location as a large map arrow, current bin status (red/amber/green),
     and a "Mark Collected" button — designed at a Standard 4 digital
     literacy level. Training consists of a 90-minute hands-on session
     in Bahasa Malaysia with printed quick-reference cards. A designated
     "SmartTONG Champion" supervisor per route handles escalations.

Q42. [REAL WORLD vs. DEMO — PROTOTYPE GAP]
     Your demo shows 4 bins in a simulated environment. What specific
     technical challenges do you anticipate when deploying to 100 real-world
     bins in a live municipal environment?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     Known anticipated challenges: (1) Network heterogeneity — bins may
     span multiple Wi-Fi/4G zones requiring SIM-based fallback; (2) Physical
     installation variability — bin sizes/materials differ across council
     areas requiring custom sensor mounting; (3) Data volume — 100 bins
     generate 14,400 readings/day requiring proper database indexing;
     (4) Edge cases — bins near food courts generating constant contamination
     alerts requiring threshold tuning. We have a 6-week pre-deployment
     site survey process to address these proactively.

Q43. [DASHBOARD DEPENDENCY — INTERNET OUTAGE]
     If your cloud infrastructure (Firebase, Vercel, Render) goes down,
     the entire SmartTONG dashboard becomes inaccessible. How do you
     ensure operational continuity for the council's waste collection
     schedule during a cloud outage?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     We implement a daily scheduled export of the optimised collection
     route as a PDF/printable route card, so truck operations can continue
     manually if the dashboard is unavailable. The ESP32 units continue
     logging locally. We have chosen Firebase for its 99.9% SLA, and
     Vercel's CDN ensures dashboard availability even if our backend
     Render service restarts. We are evaluating adding a hot-standby on
     Railway.app as a backup server.

Q47. [MARKET SIZE REALITY CHECK]

Q48. [WHAT IF AI MISCLASSIFIES — LIABILITY]
     If SmartTONG's AI tells a truck driver that a bin is not full and the
     driver skips it, but it was actually full (sensor failure), causing
     waste overflow and a public health hazard — who is legally liable?
     SmartTONG or the council?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     This is addressed in our Service Agreement. SmartTONG is a decision-

     explicitly states that SmartTONG data is advisory. We limit liability
     to the monthly subscription fee value. However, we mitigate the risk
     technically: sensor disagreements trigger a manual inspection alert
     rather than automatic skip authorisation.

Q50. [THE HARDEST QUESTION — WHY WILL THIS FAIL?]
     Every judge's ultimate question: What is the single most likely reason
     SmartTONG will fail, and what are you doing to prevent it?

     MODEL ANSWER:
     The most likely failure mode is NOT technical — it is adoption failure
     at the institutional level. Malaysian government procurement and
     organisational inertia mean that even a proven, cost-effective solution
     can stall for years in approval cycles. Our mitigation strategy is
     threefold: (1) Build credibility with a private sector pilot first
     to generate evidence that de-risks government adoption; (2) Cultivate
     internal champions within councils at the officer level (not just
     Yang Dipertua Majlis level) who will advocate from within; (3) Align
     explicitly with RMKe-12 KPIs so that SmartTONG adoption becomes a
     performance metric for council officers themselves — creating bottom-up
     institutional demand.
